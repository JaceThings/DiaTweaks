# Toolbar in sidebar

Moves the address, navigation buttons and extensions to the top of the sidebar, as in Arc, and moves the page up into the toolbar's place.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Toolbar in sidebar**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable sidebarToolbar
```

## Works with

While [Hide toolbar](hideToolbar.md) is on, this switch is greyed out in Settings and the toolbar stays hidden in the page.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I edit the address?</strong></summary>

Click it. The command bar opens over it, as it does in the toolbar.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where did Downloads and my extensions go?</strong></summary>

Downloads moves to the bottom of the sidebar, in place of the library button. Pinned extensions open from the puzzle piece in the address.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What happens in a split view?</strong></summary>

Each page keeps its own toolbar. The sidebar shows the address of the page you're using, and clicking it edits that page's address.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What about tabs at the top?</strong></summary>

There's no sidebar, so the toolbar stays above the page.

</details>

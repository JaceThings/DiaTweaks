# Hide Chats button

Hides the **Chats** button at the bottom of the sidebar, or in the tab bar when tabs are at the top.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Chats button**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideChatsButton
```
